Frock UI · the system · v1 draft

The app is your Bots' office. Chat is one room in it.

Today the app is a chat list with settings behind it. That is how every AI product looks, and it hides the thing FrockBot actually does: Bots that work while you are away, with real accounts, real Applets and a real Computer.

So the home is a briefing, not a thread list. Every action a Bot takes is a receipt you can read in a second. Bots are characters with a state you can see across the room. And the pink stops being paint and becomes light: it glows behind whichever Bot is working, and it marks the one thing that needs you.

Briefing over inboxOpen the app and read what happened, what needs you, and what is running. You never hunt through chats to find out.
Receipts, not logsEach tool call becomes a one-line receipt: icon tile, plain sentence, mono time. In chat, on the Bot's Work tab, and in the briefing.
Bots as charactersSheep avatars with a state ring: pink working, green ready, grey idle. Names set in the display face like a wordmark.
Accent as lightRadial glow behind a working Bot, a glow under the one primary pill. Everything else is tone on tone.
One universal input"Ask any Bot" lives on the briefing. Routing to the right Bot is the product's job, not yours.
Mono where it countsTimes, counts, ids and the Computer's output in JetBrains Mono. It signals precision without a terminal aesthetic.

Depth is tone. ground → window → sheet → tile → tile.2. Each step is 3 to 4% lighter and a raised surface carries a 1px top highlight at 5% white. No drop shadows except under things that float.

Ink is alpha. Three tiers of the same colour, so text sits correctly on every depth without a per-surface grey.

Accent is light. Solid for the one action per screen and the state ring. Tint for tiles, selection and chips. Ink for tinted words. Glow behind a working Bot and under the one pink pill.

Status is desaturated. Good, warn and danger have their chroma pulled down so they read without glowing; each has a 14% tint for tiles.

Light theme exists for Applets and previews: cream ground, the same names, the same tints. It is a second palette, not an inversion.

Manrope does the work at 400 and 600 only. Nothing under 20px is heavier than 600.

Motion: one curve, two durations, no bounce, no ripple. Pressed darkens one step. Sheets follow the finger.
