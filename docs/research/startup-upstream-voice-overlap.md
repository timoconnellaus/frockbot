## What remains unimplemented

- **The opening-audio gap remains.** `_beginCall` drains audio as soon as `start_call` is sent; wake sends pre-roll immediately. The new `_handshake` completes on sending, not on server admission or Gemini readiness. Server custom messages return when `#calls` has no entry, and audio returns when the session does not exist. The new sequencing therefore does not establish the plan's end-to-end readiness guarantee; delay admission and prompt preparation when testing immediate Resume, mute, pause, hang-up and audio after reconnect. Sources: [drain](../../apps/native/lib/voice/assistant.dart#L866), [wake/pre-roll](../../apps/native/lib/voice/assistant.dart#L674), [custom messages](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L1462), [audio](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L1867).
- **Prompt and Gemini connection are still serial.** `openSession` awaits prompt context, pending delegations and session Memory before constructing/starting the transport. Server cancellation/ownership checks and deadlines have not been redesigned in this diff. The existing in-memory `call.waking` coalesces wakes, but there is still no unified initial/wake/rejoin attempt spanning all these asynchronous boundaries. Sources: [open session](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L1778), [transport](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L513), [wake coalescing](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L2002).
- **Call rejoin is still different from Gemini resumption.** Upstream persists `paused`, not the provider handle. The handle still lives on `LiveCall`; releasing connection resources removes that live object. A paused rejoin builds a new live call without the handle, and waking it does not pass a ledger handover unless another existing fallback path requests one. Durable provider resumption and an explicit missing-handle continuity fallback remain planned work. Sources: [live handle](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L348), [new live call](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L1690), [resource release](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L2049), [wake](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L2012).
- **Preparation and historical recovery findings remain.** `onStart` still performs scans, retention and old deliveries; transcript sending still catches failures without a durable sender retry intent. Prompt construction still queries all Bots and repeats initial session-Memory reads. Ledger turn lookup still scans by the broad turn prefix. Paused re-admission starts `buildPromptContext` even though it then keeps Gemini closed. Sources: [startup](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L887), [transcript sender](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L1001), [context](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L3607), [directory](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L3474), [second Memory read](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L1805), [turn lookup](../../app/voice/ledger.ts#L532), [paused preparation](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L1699).

## Updates to make in the existing research and plan

1. Replace the blanket statement that the native client ends a call rather than reconnecting. It now rejoins paused/backgrounded calls; ordinary live-drop recovery and durable Gemini continuity remain incomplete. Qualify the initial microphone-readiness statement too: `_beginCall` now permits a paused/muted rejoin without capture, while the server keeps an admitted paused call asleep. See the source citations above.
2. Preserve the recovery boundary, but name the two rejoin policies and the record-derived abandonment deadline. Do not finalize a paused call after 60 seconds, reset its deadline on every activation, or let recovery treat the newly rejoined call as abandoned. Keep UTC daily allowance rollover independent of the rolling paused-call window. Sources: [window policy](../../app/voice/ledger.ts#L209), [meter day](../../app/voice/ledger.ts#L239), [alarm](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L1048).
3. Clarify “explicit hang-up must not reconnect”: it must not automatically restart conversation or paid execution, but the existing bounded control-only reconnect to deliver `end_call` must remain possible. Distinguish that operation from ordinary recovery. Source: [end](../../apps/native/lib/voice/assistant.dart#L981).
4. Keep attempt-correlated readiness. **`status: listening` alone is no longer proof Gemini is ready:** paused admission deliberately emits it without opening Gemini. A new audio-release contract must distinguish admitted/paused from setup-complete/ready and retain pause/mute intent across reconnect. Source: [paused admission](../../apps/cloudflare/src/voice-assistant.ts#L1745).
5. Add the classifier and chime to preservation requirements, not to the backend optimization list. Preserve asynchronous classifier preparation, fallback, reset/disposal, AEC-dependent barge-in and uninterrupted awake streaming. Keep the local chime once per call and separate from reply playback; do not count it as the first model response. Sources: [frame handling](../../apps/native/lib/voice/assistant.dart#L634), [classifier lifecycle](../../apps/native/lib/voice/speech_classifier_io.dart#L34), [chime trigger](../../apps/native/lib/voice/assistant.dart#L749).

Existing added tests cover paused rejoin after ten minutes, expiry after twenty-five hours, restoring the short policy on wake, background/paused client rejoin, hang-up after a dropped pause, classifier-controlled wake/barge-in and the once-per-call chime. Keep them as baseline acceptance. They do not establish an admission/setup readiness barrier under delayed server work. Extend the already agreed fake-based matrix with delayed `start_call` admission followed immediately by Resume/end/mute, no-metered-upstream paused rejoin, expiry and replacement races, and classifier-not-ready/failure during opening. Sources: [ledger tests](../../app/voice/ledger.test.ts#L130), [server rejoin tests](../../apps/cloudflare/test/voice-assistant.workerd.ts#L899), [client rejoin tests](../../apps/native/test/voice_assistant_test.dart#L591), [opening-buffer test](../../apps/native/test/voice_assistant_test.dart#L142), [classifier tests](../../apps/native/test/voice_assistant_test.dart#L702), [chime test](../../apps/native/test/voice_assistant_test.dart#L106).

No new startup measurements or live-model verification are needed for this overlap decision. It changes the baseline and acceptance constraints, not the agreed architecture or implementation deferral.
