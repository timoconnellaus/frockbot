How the split is organised

  • Exactly seven controls: two eyes, two ears, two feet, and one fur/body group.
  • The face and Guardian’s horns stay with the fur/body. Hair and wool are continuous.
  • Ears and feet are complete closed shapes. The legs extend far inside the fur, with their pivots at the buried upper ends.
  • Eyes are smooth capsules. Hiding them reveals a solid face; hiding Sunny’s ears reveals continuous yellow wool.
  • Every asset is an editable vector. No clipping masks, raster patches, or animation.

The leg roots remain covered in nine static poses per leg: up to 12° each way and 20 units up or down. This checks overlap; no animation has been added.
