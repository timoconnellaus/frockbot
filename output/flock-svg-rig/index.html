What “95%” means here

The score is the lowest of foreground RGB agreement, masked local structural similarity (SSIM), and coloured-edge F1 within one source pixel. Every measure must exceed 95%. The reference mask and crop are fixed before tracing. Empty card background and headings do not contribute to the score. This is a reproducible image-comparison target, not a claim that perception can be reduced to one number.

Inspect the differences

Overlay reveals alignment. Difference maps amplify RGB differences ×6: black is agreement, brighter pixels differ. Detail zoom shows the same fixed area of both images. SVGs must contain vector paths only—no embedded PNGs or image elements. The original source and all revisions remain available.
