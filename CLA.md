## How to sign

Open a pull request. The CLA check comments on it; reply with a new comment (not a quote-reply) containing exactly:

> I have read the FrockBot CLA version 1.0 and I hereby sign it.

Your GitHub username, the pull request and the time are recorded, and you will not be asked again for this version. If nothing happens, comment `recheck`.

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must also agree: they post the same sentence on the pull request from their own GitHub account, adding "on behalf of @yourusername", or email the Maintainer.

If your employer, or a client you are contracting for, has rights in your contribution, they sign the [Corporate CLA](CCLA.md) and list you under it instead. You then do not sign here: once you are listed, your pull requests pass the check.

Pull requests that change fewer than five lines in total pass the check without a signature.
