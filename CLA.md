## How to sign

Open a pull request. The CLA check comments on it; reply with a new comment (not a quote-reply) consisting of only this sentence, with nothing before or after it:

> I have read the FrockBot CLA version 1.0 and I hereby sign it.

Your GitHub username, the pull request and the time are recorded, and you will not be asked again for this version. If nothing happens, comment `recheck`.

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must also email the Maintainer, with your GitHub username, consenting to you signing. You still sign here yourself.

If your employer, or a client you are contracting for, owns or has rights in your contribution, they also sign the [Corporate CLA](CCLA.md) and list you under it, unless they authorise you in writing to sign for them. You still sign here yourself.

A pull request that changes fewer than five lines of text in total, and adds or changes no binary or generated file, passes the check without a signature. It is submitted under the MIT License.
