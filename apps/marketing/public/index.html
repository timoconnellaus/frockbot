Before you start

Fair questions.

Is it safe with my email?

Your bots reach only the apps you connect, and every step they take is written down for you to read. Protected actions ask you first. The code is open, so anyone can check how it works.

What if it gets something wrong?

Tell it, the way you'd tell a person. It remembers how you like things done, and you can see exactly what it did.

I already pay for ChatGPT. Why this?

Connect it and your bots use it. FrockBot adds what a chat window can't: memory for each job, routines that run while you're out, a computer of its own, and your apps.

Can it spend my money?

It doesn't buy things for you. It finds the deal and sends you the link. Its own running costs come from your balance, which you can see and cap for each bot, and which pauses rather than overcharging.

What happens if I cancel?

Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, and your conversations stay yours to read afterwards.
