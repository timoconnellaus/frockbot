Privacy Policy.

This policy explains what information FrockBot handles, why it is needed, and the choices available to you.

Effective: 28 August 2026

In a nutshell

FrockBot is operated by Tim O'Connell. The hosted service keeps bots, sessions, memory, and files so they can remain useful between visits. Optional desktop integrations have their own data flows.

privacy@frockbot.com

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies when you visit frockbot.com, use the hosted FrockBot app at bot.frockbot.com, or use a FrockBot desktop client with those services. “FrockBot”, “we”, and “us” mean the service operated by Tim O'Connell.

FrockBot is also open-source software. If you run your own copy, the person operating that copy is responsible for its data practices; this policy does not describe their deployment.

2. Information FrockBot handles

Sign-in and profile information

FrockBot uses Google sign-in. We receive the information needed to identify you and create your FrockBot user, such as your Google account identifier, name, email address, profile image, and sign-in status. Authentication records may include encrypted OAuth tokens, session tokens, sign-in dates, IP address, and user-agent details.

Your bots, sessions, memory, and files

The hosted app stores the content you provide and the records it needs to make bots persistent. This can include bot identifiers and configuration, your inputs, ordered session events, model requests and responses, tool calls and results, routines, standing memory, and files you or a bot add to memory. Memory content is split into chunks and converted into numerical embeddings so relevant material can be found later.

Technical and support information

We may handle request and device details needed to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the service, including IP address, browser or app details, timestamps, authentication state, and error or diagnostic information. If you contact us, we handle your contact details and whatever you include in the correspondence.

3. How information is used

We use information to:

  • authenticate you and keep your sessions working;
  • run bots, preserve session history, search memory, and store files;
  • send requested material to models and tools and return their results;
  • operate, secure, debug, and improve FrockBot;
  • respond to questions, privacy requests, and disputes; and
  • comply with applicable law and protect users, FrockBot, and others.

4. Hosted FrockBot and Cloudflare

The FrockBot app runs on Cloudflare. Its current hosted architecture uses Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects to run the app and keep bot session state, D1 to store authentication records, R2 to store persistent memory files and application artefacts, Vectorize to index memory embeddings, and Workers AI to create those embeddings. Cloudflare therefore processes hosted-service information on our behalf as part of providing this infrastructure.

These hosted stores are separate from data held by an optional desktop integration. Using the website alone does not give FrockBot access to arbitrary files on your computer.

5. AI models and tools

A bot may send information needed for a turn to an AI model provider. Depending on your setup, that can include instructions, relevant session history and memory, your input, tool descriptions, and tool results. A configured OpenAI-compatible provider receives requests at the endpoint selected for that desktop runtime. The provider handles that information under its own terms and privacy practices.

In the hosted service, Cloudflare Workers AI receives memory text to produce embeddings used by memory search. Installable packages and tools can introduce additional data flows; review a package and its configuration before enabling it.

6. Optional desktop and Fly Sprite integration

Desktop FrockBot can optionally be configured with a Fly.io Sprite, a remote persistent computer. When you enable it, the desktop-hosted integration uses the configured Fly Sprites token and sends commands and browser actions to Fly. The Sprite can retain a bot's workspace, files, browser profile, standing memory, and a mirrored session record on its persistent filesystem. Its browser can also communicate with sites you direct it to.

Sprite storage is not the same store as hosted R2, Vectorize, or Durable Object data, and the repository does not automatically synchronise those stores. Local desktop settings, selected folders, clipboard content, and notifications are handled by the desktop and its operating-system permissions unless a feature, package, or your instruction sends that information to the hosted service, an AI provider, a Sprite, or another destination.

7. When information is disclosed

Information may be disclosed:

  • to Google for sign-in;
  • to Cloudflare for the hosted app and storage described above;
  • to an AI provider or tool when it is used for your bot;
  • to Fly.io when you configure and use the optional Sprite integration;
  • when you direct or authorise the disclosure;
  • when required by law or a valid legal process; or
  • to protect rights, safety, security, and the integrity of the service.

These services may process information outside Australia. Their own terms and privacy practices also apply to their processing.

8. Cookies and sign-in storage

FrockBot uses cookies or equivalent app storage where needed to keep you signed in, protect authenticated requests, and remember essential app state. Blocking that storage may stop sign-in or other parts of the service from working.

9. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as it is needed to provide and secure FrockBot, maintain persistent bots and sessions, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. What is needed depends on the type of information and how the service is used. You can contact us about deletion; we will assess requests under applicable law and any operational or legal requirements.

We use technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the service, including access controls and encryption of stored OAuth tokens. No internet or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your choices and privacy rights

You choose what content to give a bot and whether to enable packages, model endpoints, or the Fly Sprite integration. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to ask for access to or correction of personal information, or to make a privacy complaint. Email us to make a request. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

11. Children

FrockBot is not designed for children who cannot lawfully consent to the handling described in this policy. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information should contact us.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as FrockBot changes. We will publish the updated version here and change its effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or seek consent.
