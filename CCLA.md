## How to sign

Email the Maintainer to ask for this Agreement as a document to sign. It is signed by a person authorised to bind the organisation, and it lists the Designated Contributors' names, emails and GitHub usernames in Schedule A. Once it is countersigned, those usernames are added to the CLA check, and their pull requests pass it.
