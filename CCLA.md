# FrockBot Corporate Contributor Licence Agreement ("Agreement")

Version 1.0. The text of a version is never changed: any change, however small, is published as a new version.

_In short (this summary is not part of the Agreement): an organisation whose employees or contractors contribute to FrockBot signs this to license whatever it owns in their contributions, on the same terms as the [Individual CLA](CLA.md). Each person it lists still signs the Individual CLA for themselves, which covers anything of theirs the organisation does not own, and their moral rights._

In consideration of the Maintainer's promises in sections 3 and 10 of the Individual CLA as they apply under this Agreement, and of the Maintainer considering Your Contributions for inclusion in the Work, You accept and agree to the following terms and conditions for the Contributions submitted to the Maintainer by Your Designated Contributors. Except for the licences and consents granted in this Agreement, You reserve all right, title, and interest in and to Your Contributions.

1. **Definitions and interpretation.**

   "Individual CLA" shall mean the FrockBot Individual Contributor Licence Agreement, version 1.0, published as CLA.md in the Public Repository.

   "You" (or "Your") shall mean the legal entity named in Schedule A that signs this Agreement.

   "Designated Contributor" shall mean an individual listed in Schedule A, as updated under section 5.

   "Maintainer", "Project", "Public Repository" and "Work" have the meanings given in the Individual CLA.

   "Contribution" shall mean any original work of authorship, including any modifications or additions to an existing work, that You own or have rights in, and that a Designated Contributor intentionally submits to the Maintainer for inclusion in, or documentation of, the Work, while listed in Schedule A. "Submitted" has the meaning given in the Individual CLA.

   Where this Agreement applies a section of the Individual CLA, that section is read as part of this Agreement: "You", "Your" and "Contribution" have the meanings given in this Agreement, "submitted by You" includes submitted by a Designated Contributor, and a reference in it to another numbered section means the section of this Agreement with that number.

2. **Grant of Copyright Licence.** Section 2 of the Individual CLA applies to Your Contributions.

3. **Outbound licence.** Section 3 of the Individual CLA applies to Your Contributions.

4. **Grant of Patent Licence.** Section 4 of the Individual CLA applies to Your Contributions. If a Designated Contributor is employed or engaged by an entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with You, You represent that You are authorised to grant the licences in this Agreement for that Designated Contributor's Contributions on that entity's behalf; "control" means the power to direct an entity's management or policies, or ownership of more than half its voting shares.

5. **Designated Contributors.** You authorise each Designated Contributor to submit Contributions on Your behalf. Each Designated Contributor also signs the Individual CLA for themselves. You will tell the Maintainer in writing when a person should be added to or removed from Schedule A; a removal does not affect Contributions submitted before it.

6. **Authority and original work.** You represent that You are legally entitled to grant the licences in this Agreement, and that the person signing it for You is authorised to do so. Section 6 of the Individual CLA applies to Your Contributions, with "the original creation of a Designated Contributor" in place of "Your original creation".

7. **Support and warranty.** Section 7 of the Individual CLA applies to Your Contributions.

8. **Work of others.** Section 8 of the Individual CLA applies to work that is not the original creation of a Designated Contributor.

9. **Notice of change.** Section 9 of the Individual CLA applies to this Agreement.

10. **Assignment.** Section 10 of the Individual CLA applies to this Agreement.

11. **Moral rights.** Moral rights stay with the individuals who author a Contribution. Each Designated Contributor gives the consents in section 11 of the Individual CLA when they sign it.

12. **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia, and the parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of its courts.

13. **New versions and ending.** Section 13 of the Individual CLA applies to this Agreement, with "Your Designated Contributors submit" in place of "You submit".

14. **General.** Section 14 of the Individual CLA applies to this Agreement, except its last two sentences. A Designated Contributor's own Individual CLA applies to the rights they hold, and this Agreement to the rights You hold.

## How to sign

Email the Maintainer to ask for this Agreement as a document to sign. It is signed by a person authorised to bind the organisation, and lists the Designated Contributors' names, emails and GitHub usernames in Schedule A. Each Designated Contributor also signs the Individual CLA on their first pull request. The Maintainer keeps the countersigned Agreement and every Schedule A update.
